                      Keys

                        Click a key, then press the new key or a mouse button. Esc cancels. The arrow keys, right Shift and C also work until you give them to something else, and the mouse wheel always jumps.

                        WASDMove. In the air, strafe with A or D while turning the mouse the same way.
                        Space / wheelJump. Hold to hop. Tap again mid-wallrun or mid-climb to kick off.
                        Shift / CCrouch. Press while running fast, or hold it as you land, to slide.
                        Into a wallJump at a wall head-on to climb it. Hold W and run past one at an angle to wallrun.
                        Into a ledgeHold W to mantle. Jump into a low obstacle to vault it at speed.
                        1 to 0 / TJump straight to a level, or the tutorial.
                        Crouch on landingRoll out of a big drop.
                        R / FRestart level, last checkpoint.
                        PPractice mode. Z saves your exact spot and speed, X (or F, or falling) puts you back. Practice runs are not recorded.
                        M / EscMute sound. Open this menu. In the menu, arrow keys pick a level and Enter plays it.
                        ControllerLeft stick moves, right stick looks. A or LB jumps (hold to hop), B, LT or right stick click crouches. X back to checkpoint, View restarts, Menu pauses. In menus, D-pad moves, A picks, B goes back. Auto strafe (Settings) presses A or D in the air as you turn.
